Our Story

Welcome to Chatalog,
your book recommendation chatbot.

Your Library. Your Recommendations. A chatbot that can answer it all.

We are a group of Information Resource Management students at Carleton University with a vision of improving user access to the Seattle Public Library resources. Currently, library websites do not provide a method of searching for book recommendations using artificial intelligence. Whether for research or leisure, our innovative system can recommend your next read from the SPL's catalog by searching for similar books based on title, author, or keyword.

The project combines the team's shared background in library science, information management, and information technology with innovative developments in AI-driven search and recommendation systems. Chatalog is an innovative system with the application of emerging technology, combining retrieval augmented generation (RAG), knowledge graphs, and large language models (LLMs) which creates a unique approach to the analysis and interaction with library catalogs. Our chatbot provides personalized, context-aware book recommendations beyond basic catalog searches with an interactive interface for exploring a library's collection.

Throughout the exploration of your library resources, Chatalog will be there to help you!
